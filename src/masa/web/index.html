Detection confidence

    
The minimum person-detection score accepted by the pose model. Raising it rejects less-confident person detections; lowering it may include more people and more false positives.

    
It is not a joint-confidence threshold and does not identify the intended fighters. Maximum people caps saved observations. Manual box selection chooses the intended targets; the automatic confidence cap can still select spectators.

  
  
    
Keypoint confidence

    
The minimum joint score used for reliable skeleton connections and derived 2D measurements. Lower-confidence joints may remain as hollow markers; unreliable measurements stay missing.

    
This does not remove background people or change which person detections are accepted. Use Detection confidence for person acceptance. A higher joint score is not proof of correct anatomy.
